Questions for your mentor. Saved on this phone and in backups.
1. Śrī Guru praṇāma
oṁ ajñāna-timirāndhasya jñānāñjana-śalākayā
cakṣur unmīlitaṁ yena tasmai śrī-gurave namaḥ
om ag-ya-na ti-mi-ran-dhas-ya gya-nan-ja-na sha-la-ka-ya
chak-shur un-mee-li-tam ye-na tas-mai shree-gu-ra-ve na-mah
A bow to the spiritual teacher, who opens the eyes with the lamp of knowledge.
2. Śrīla Prabhupāda praṇāma
nama oṁ viṣṇu-pādāya kṛṣṇa-preṣṭhāya bhū-tale
śrīmate bhaktivedānta-svāmin iti nāmine
na-ma om vish-nu pa-da-ya krish-na presh-tha-ya bhoo-ta-lay
shree-ma-tay bhak-ti-ve-dan-ta sva-min i-ti na-mi-nay
namas te sārasvate deve gaura-vāṇī-pracāriṇe
nirviśeṣa-śūnyavādi-pāścātya-deśa-tāriṇe
na-mas tay sa-ras-va-tay day-vay gau-ra va-nee pra-cha-ri-nay
nir-vi-she-sha shoon-ya-va-di pa-shcha-tya day-sha ta-ri-nay
A bow to Śrīla Prabhupāda, your teacher in this line, who brought these teachings to the wider world.
3. Vaiṣṇava praṇāma
vāñchā-kalpatarubhyaś ca kṛpā-sindhubhya eva ca
patitānāṁ pāvanebhyo vaiṣṇavebhyo namo namaḥ
van-cha kal-pa-ta-rub-hyash cha kri-pa sin-dhub-hya e-va cha
pa-ti-ta-nam pa-va-neb-hyo vaish-na-veb-hyo na-mo na-mah
Respects to the Vaiṣṇavas, kind like wish-fulfilling trees and oceans of mercy, who lift up those who have fallen.
4. Pañca-tattva mantra
(jaya) śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya prabhu nityānanda
śrī-advaita gadādhara śrīvāsādi-gaura-bhakta-vṛnda
jay-uh shree-krish-na chai-tan-ya pra-bhoo nit-ya-nan-da
shree-ad-vai-ta ga-da-dha-ra shree-va-sa-di gau-ra bhak-ta vrin-da
Chant once before beginning rounds. A short address to Śrī Caitanya and His close associates.
5. Hare Krishna mahā-mantra
Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare Kṛṣṇa
Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Hare Hare
Hare Rāma Hare Rāma
Rāma Rāma Hare Hare
Ha-ray Krish-na Ha-ray Krish-na
Krish-na Krish-na Ha-ray Ha-ray
Ha-ray Ra-ma Ha-ray Ra-ma
Ra-ma Ra-ma Ha-ray Ha-ray
The holy names of Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa. One full mantra per bead, 108 beads to a round.
6. Śrī Caitanya praṇāma
namo mahā-vadānyāya kṛṣṇa-prema-pradāya te
kṛṣṇāya kṛṣṇa-caitanya-nāmne gaura-tviṣe namaḥ
na-mo ma-ha va-dan-ya-ya krish-na pre-ma pra-da-ya tay
krish-na-ya krish-na chai-tan-ya nam-nay gau-ra tvish-e na-mah
A bow to Śrī Caitanya, Kṛṣṇa appearing in a golden form, freely giving love of God.
7. Śrī Kṛṣṇa praṇāma
he kṛṣṇa karuṇā-sindho dīna-bandho jagat-pate
gopeśa gopikā-kānta rādhā-kānta namo ’stu te
hay krish-na ka-ru-na sin-dho dee-na ban-dho ja-gat pa-tay
go-pay-sha go-pi-ka kan-ta ra-dha kan-ta na-mo stu tay
A bow to Kṛṣṇa, the ocean of kindness, friend of the distressed, and beloved of the gopīs and of Rādhā.
8. Śrī Rādhā praṇāma
tapta-kāñcana-gaurāṅgi rādhe vṛndāvaneśvari
vṛṣabhānu-sute devi praṇamāmi hari-priye
tap-ta kan-cha-na gau-ran-gi ra-dhay vrin-da-va-nay-shva-ri
vri-sha-bha-nu su-tay day-vi pra-na-ma-mi ha-ri pri-yay
A bow to Śrī Rādhā, shining like molten gold, the queen of Vṛndāvana and Kṛṣṇa’s dearmost.
9. Tulasī praṇāma
vṛndāyai tulasī-devyai priyāyai keśavasya ca
kṛṣṇa-bhakti-prade devi satya-vatyai namo namaḥ
vrin-da-yai tu-la-see dayv-yai pri-ya-yai kay-sha-vas-ya cha
krish-na bhak-ti pra-day day-vi sat-ya-vat-yai na-mo na-mah
A bow to Tulasī-devī, dear to Kṛṣṇa, who grants devotion to Him.
10. Nṛsiṁha praṇāma
namas te nara-siṁhāya prahlādāhlāda-dāyine
hiraṇyakaśipor vakṣaḥ-śilā-ṭaṅka-nakhālaye
na-mas tay na-ra sim-ha-ya prah-la-dah-la-da da-yi-nay
hi-ran-ya-ka-shi-por vak-shah shi-la tan-ka na-kha-la-ye
ito nṛsiṁhaḥ parato nṛsiṁho yato yato yāmi tato nṛsiṁhaḥ
bahir nṛsiṁho hṛdaye nṛsiṁho nṛsiṁham ādiṁ śaraṇaṁ prapadye
i-to nri-sim-hah pa-ra-to nri-sim-ho ya-to ya-to ya-mi ta-to nri-sim-hah
ba-hir nri-sim-ho hri-da-ye nri-sim-ho nri-sim-ham a-dim sha-ra-nam pra-pad-ye
A bow to Lord Nṛsiṁha, who delighted Prahlāda and is shelter on every side, outside and in the heart.
11. Offering prayer
namo brahmaṇya-devāya go-brāhmaṇa-hitāya ca
jagad-dhitāya kṛṣṇāya govindāya namo namaḥ
na-mo brah-man-ya day-va-ya go-brah-ma-na hi-ta-ya cha
ja-gad dhi-ta-ya krish-na-ya go-vin-da-ya na-mo na-mah
Used when offering food to Kṛṣṇa, who cares for the cows, the brāhmaṇas, and the whole world. Said after the Prabhupāda praṇāma when offering bhoga.

A hymn of eight verses praising the spiritual master, sung in the early-morning ārati.
Morning altar sequence
  1. Wake and shower
  2. Clean the altar
  3. Offer obeisances
  4. Offer water (fresh water cup / ācamana)
  5. Pañca-tattva mantra and prayers
  6. Japa: morning rounds
  7. Bhagavad Gītā study
Evening: final rounds, offer obeisances, bed at 10:00 PM. The Dāmodara lamp was daily during Kārtik (through Nov 24).
Om Tat Sat: offering a meal
Before eating, place the plate before Kṛṣṇa (or simply hold it with gratitude), offer it with a short prayer, and close with “oṁ tat sat”, dedicating the meal to the Supreme. Then honor it as prasādam.
Mark each offered meal on the Japa tab. The offering prayer above is the verse used at this step.
